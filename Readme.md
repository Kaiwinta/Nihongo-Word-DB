## Word of the day

### 🇯🇵 引札
- **Reading:** ひきふだ
- **Translation:** announcement flier (e.g. sale at a shop), raffle ticket, lottery ticket, hikifuda (type of playing cards numbered 1-6, used by the bank in tehonbiki), kurifuda
- **Type:** noun
